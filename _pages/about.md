I am currently a Lecturer in the Department of Mathematics, School of Artificial Intelligence and Big Data, Xi'an University, since September 2026.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Jilin University in June 2026, under the supervision of Prof. Bin Guo and Prof. Huilai Li. I obtained my Master's degree in Applied Mathematics from Jilin University, under the supervision of Prof. Bin Guo, and my Bachelor's degree in Information and Computing Science from Tiangong University.

**Research Interests**

My research interests include the well-posedness, long-time behavior, and blow-up properties of weak solutions to fourth- and higher-order parabolic equations.
